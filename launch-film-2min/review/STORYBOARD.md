## Rules this cut follows
- **Impact language only in the battle** (and its echo in the supercut): the four biggest hits get the micro-shake (title, Hit, the last ship, Victory).
- **No slow-mo** (all footage is 30 fps): freeze-frame + push-in instead (Aim, the heartbeat, Victory).
- **Type never sits over busy UI**: the plate is dimmed/blurred (title, heartbeat, Victory), shaded (Aim/Fire/Hit, weapon labels) or the type is on black (everything else).
- **Honesty**: every name and number is from the code (`app.json`, `ARSENAL_NAMES`, `STORE_ITEMS`, `FLEET_SHIP_COUNT`). No wallet address, QR or signature is readable. The profile screen (email, Privy ID) is not used. UI text such as "Wager won" is never called out.

## Differences from the brief's starting storyboard
- **Cold open 8 s, not 6 s**, so both lines get their full reading time (≥ 0.4 s/word + 0.6 s) and the bit can travel into the period. The title is 6 s; the total is still 1:54.
- **"Gear up." → "Collect every colour."** The store sells colour editions for the collection only; nothing in the game reads them (CLAUDE.md, `src/features/store`). "Gear up." would promise a gameplay advantage that doesn't exist.
- **Port art** appears as a bento tile ("Port City"), not in "Build your base". The Port City is a preview with no building system, so "build" copy stays on the real fleet + defence placement.
- **Battle montage inserts use the game's own FX strips** (Radar, Submarine, Mine) on graph paper, because the recordings only contain the Atomic Bomber, the Bomber and the AA gun.
- **Motion blur on the whip-pans** is a horizontal SVG blur driven by the pan speed. `@remotion/motion-blur`'s HTML-in-canvas mode needs Chrome ≥ 149; the sandbox's headless shell is older.
