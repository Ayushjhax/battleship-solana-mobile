## Logo
No vector logo exists. Raster options: `demo-assets/logo.png` (1200², key art "Empire of Bits BATTLESHIP" on a painted scene), `assets/images/battle-screen/empire-ocean-logo.png` (597×231 banner), `assets/images/background/logo.png` (595×237).
